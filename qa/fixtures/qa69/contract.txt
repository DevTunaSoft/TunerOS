The rows describe a bounded scheduled-recording preparation set. Only rows in
PREPARING or RECOVERING are orphaned work. Enumeration is ordered by id, is
exclusive of the after id and is bounded by the page cap. A claim may move only
an inert reservation with the same id and scratch source marker into RECOVERING.
Deletion removes only a RECOVERING row with the same scratch source marker.
Diagnostics carry check ids, counts and digests only.
